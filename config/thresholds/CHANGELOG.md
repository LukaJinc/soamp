## v0.2.0 - 2026-11-13

Added `dev_dbaasp_thresholds.csv` (separate table for the `data/dev/` dataset; the legacy
`organism_thresholds.csv` is untouched). E. coli / S. aureus / P. aeruginosa: active 25, inactive 100,
**in ug/ml** (dev `activity` is DBAASP's ug/ml-normalised value; the `*_uM` column names are legacy).
Source: DBAASP prediction tools (https://dbaasp.org/prediction/genome): Active MIC<25 ug/ml,
Non-Active MIC>100 ug/ml, applied strictly (exactly 25 or 100 -> uncertain).

Research note: no literature source was found for the earlier 32/128 values (recorded only as
"user-specified 2026-08-15"); 25/100 is the closest published precedent, also cited as the
"<25 / >100 ug/mL" positive/negative scheme of earlier classification studies in Yan et al. 2023
(mSystems, PMC10506472 -- cited statement checked, original studies not read). Other works use their
own cutoffs (e.g. 15 uM for an E. coli classifier); there is no universal AMP standard, and CLSI/EUCAST
breakpoints are for conventional antibiotics. The dev dataset previously built with 32/128 had
8,868 active / 3,137 inactive (12,005 pairs).
